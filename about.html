Public Good Protocol

Social media engineered for human resonance, not algorithmic outrage.

For twenty years, social technology was treated as an advertising optimization machine. Algorithms learned that provoking resentment generated more ad impressions than quiet discovery. Vesper is the antithesis.

1. Completely Free Forever ($0 Always)

We have eliminated all subscription gates, paywalls, and paid checkmarks. Communication and art are foundational public goods.

2. Chronological Sovereignty

You see posts in the order they were published. No mystery engagement algorithms hiding your friends or inflating controversy.

3. Lossless Media Fidelity

Photography, 35mm film scans, and audio remain uncompressed. What you capture with your camera lens is what your audience views.

4. Democratic Jury Governance

Moderation decisions in public spaces are arbitrated by rotating community juries with a public transparent appeal ledger.
