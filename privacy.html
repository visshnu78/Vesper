Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026 | Domain: vespersocial.org

1. Overview and Core Commitment

Vesper is an independent chronological platform engineered for creators and photographers. Our core architecture is built upon data minimization. We do not sell user data, run third-party advertising trackers, or construct behavioral surveillance profiles. You retain complete ownership of all media uploaded to your account.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide authentic platform services:

  • Account Registration: Username, display name, verified email address, and cryptographic password derivation parameters.
  • Profile Details: Optional biography, avatar image, and external website link supplied by you.
  • Content & Media: Photographs, captions, category tags, circle affiliations, and timestamps of publication.
  • Interactions: Likes, bookmarks, comments, circle memberships, and direct peer messages.
  • Technical Logs: Server access timestamps and IP addresses retained strictly for fraud prevention, rate limiting, and security audits for up to 30 days.

3. Password Encryption & Security Standards

We do not store passwords in plaintext or via weak algorithms. User passwords are protected using salted key-derivation functions (scrypt / PBKDF2) with cryptographically unique random salts per account. Password comparisons are executed using constant-time algorithms to eliminate timing side-channel vulnerabilities.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Vesper uses strictly necessary local browser storage solely to preserve your active session token and theme preference (light or dark mode). We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or surveillance pixels.

5. Data Portability & Account Erasure

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Access & Export: You can download a complete, structured JSON export of your profile, media links, and interaction history at any time from your Account Settings.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request full deletion of your account and associated media directly in Account Settings or by contacting privacy@vespersocial.org.

6. Contact and Inquiries

For privacy inquiries, cryptographic security reports, or data erasure requests, contact our designated Data Protection Officer at:

privacy@vespersocial.org
